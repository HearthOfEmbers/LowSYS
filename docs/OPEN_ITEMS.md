## For the physics lead

From the M4 validation (engine c4e1f17; tests in `packages/lowsys_engine/test/validation/`, numbers from the engine unless marked "hand"):

- **VC-16.6c target is stale.** 0.966 bar(a) is what UA_clean 28.5 kW/K (the v1.0 70 % design) gives. With the specified 29.9 kW/K, 100 % CW gives 29.9/1.0861 = 27.53 kW/K × (T_sat − 30 °C), so ≈ 1281 kW needs T_sat ≈ 76.5 °C, the bubble point of x_D ≈ 0.995 at ≈ 0.906 bar(a) (hand). Engine 0.9056 at dt 0.5, 2 and 30 s; V₁ stays below 1.1 × steady. The test asserts 0.906 ± 0.005. Also the "70 → 100 %" start is no longer the design OP (50 %).
- **VC-8.1 (T2) / VC-16.6 "P held at SP ± 0.2 kPa".** True at the new steady state only (engine: back at SP, CW peak below 95 %). The transient peak is ≈ 2.5 kPa, and it has to be: +6.7 % duty needs ≈ +36 % CW, and with Kc 10 / Ti 120 s on the 202.65 kPa span the integral needs ≈ 875 kPa·s of error for that (hand). Suggest "returns to SP ± 0.2 kPa; transient ≤ 5 kPa". The test asserts that.
- **F11 numbers assume P held** (PHYSICS.md §11 row, §H H2). The 50 % condenser design loses its +7.9 % reserve at y_I ≈ 0.02 (blanketing 3 y_I plus the lower condensable partial pressure), so at s = 1 the CW valve saturates after ≈ 17 min and P rises: at 1 h P 139 kPa, overhead 138.7 mol (128.7 condensable + 10.0 inert), y_I 0.072, extra subcooling 20·3·y_I = 4.3 K. The engine matches every model relation exactly (n_I = ε_I F t, P = n R T₀/V, ΔT_sub = 20 f). H2's "subcooling up (≥ 5 K)" is reached only after ≈ 1 h at s = 1. Restate the row as a transient, or give the 1-h numbers at the risen P.
- **F15 Q_max figures are at the E = 1 point.** 1437 / 1070 / 384 kW use T_B = 110.4 °C (VC-11, E = 1, where §8.2 sizes UA_R). At the E = 0.7 default (T_B 109.6 °C), s = 0.5 gives 1490 kW. The engine matches the doc at the E = 1 point in the first step after the fault: 1436.5, 1070.5, 383.8 kW, limit onset between s 0.55 and 0.6. Once the duty is limited, T_B falls and Q_max recovers (s 0.6: 1299 kW after 15 min; s 0.85 on the default preset: ≈ 1200 kW after 15 min), so "limited" is an onset statement. Also, F15 alone can never raise `model.dutyLimited`: Q_v = Q_max,0 g(p) uses the clean maximum at the current header, so Q_v ≤ Q_max without fouling. Only `FC-103.OPSAT` shows. H4's "duty limited" for F15 should name OPSAT.
- **VC-27.3 note "At 0.5 bar(a) the condenser cannot condense with any CW the levers allow (≥ 5 °C)" is false.** At CW 5 °C the default condenser gives 27.53 × (59.02 − 5) ≈ 1487 kW against ≈ 1330 kW needed (hand), and the engine holds 0.5 bar(a) there (test). The limit is CW ≤ ≈ 10.7 °C (or more CW capacity). G8 is unaffected.
- **VC-11 further references need P held.** L0 150 needs Q_C 1707 kW, more than the default condenser's 1391 kW at 1 atm (VC-27.3), so P floats. The test now gives each row a CW inlet temperature at which the reference Q_C needs 50 % CW (the §7.2 design rule), as does VC-27.2. With that, D 50.00 / 55.00 and every x match. Say "perfect condenser" in VC-11 as in VC-27.2. The D 55 doc values (x_D 0.908433, Q_R 1362.4 kW) are confirmed by the MESH reference with continuation in D.
- **H9.12 "within 20 min" (spec decision #5) is marginal.** LC-101 (Ti 1800 s) needs ≈ 15 min to move the true D by 2.5 kmol/h, and the 600-s window lags that. The frozen reading also keeps the noise of the instant it froze, so the signal is 2.5 ± ≈ 1 kmol/h against a 2.1 kmol/h band. C1/C3 ≥ `check` held 60 s at 1225 / 555 / 1235 s (Tier-1 stand-in, seeds 1–3), and at 360 / 125 / 1315 s in the Tier-2 drill configuration (full hydraulics, TC-101 on, CAS-slave event). Suggest 30 min. The test asserts 30 min and reports the 20-min result.
- **Engine-side spec changes awaiting a decision.** E47 (PC-101 gain × OP/35 below 35 % OP, floor 0.1) is the gain scheduling that the M5 item above says needs a physics decision. It is already in the engine for every preset. E48 re-ranges PT-101 to 2 × max(P_design, SP) and moves the PT-101 alarms to 1.15 / 0.85 × SP. Ratify both or replace them.
- **M6: Tier-2 healthy signal looks.** The real H9/H10 drill (Tier 2, full hydraulics, TC-101 on, noise on) gives, over 1 h with no fault: FT-201 2.4–8.4, TI-202 3.2–14.7, PT-101 1.2–3.8, TI-100 0.7–2.4 (σ_5min/typicalSigma). Even with PC-101 in MAN, FT-201 reads 1.3–2.7 and TI-202 1.0–3.5 (true P σ ≈ 0.15 kPa). These tags have no loop or valve-disturbance term in their typical σ, unlike FT-104/105. H9.0's "every tag in 0.5–2" will fail in Tier 2 unless their typical σ is widened or they are excluded.

- **VC-28.1 x_B.** The [P] value is 0.012590 / T_B 96.496 °C. A re-run of the independent Newton MESH solve during the M5 pass gave 0.012564 / 96.502 °C, which is inside the 2×10⁻⁴ tolerance. The likely cause is a difference in feed temperature or ΔP handling between the two prototypes. Reconcile it once the engine's number exists.
